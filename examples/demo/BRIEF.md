# Art brief

KILN's demo: two toy worlds in one style, to show what text-made models look like.

## The game

- **Title / working name:** KILN demo
- **Genre and core loop:** none (a showcase)
- **Camera:** third person, close enough to see faces
- **Style:** soft_toy (see STYLE.md)
- **Inspirations:** Dear Passengers, Fall Guys (soft rounded toys, bright colours, soft light)

## Characters and key assets

- **Pilot** (`models/pilot.kiln.yaml`): red bean crew member, 1.15 m, cap with the airline's wings (decal), striped
  tie; cap is detachable; walk (waddle), wave, idle. Variant `copilot` (teal).
- **Passenger** (`models/passenger.kiln.yaml`): big-headed caricature in a dotted holiday shirt; walk, nod.
- **Seat** (`models/seat.kiln.yaml`): airline seat.
- **Knight**, **archer** (`models/knight|archer.kiln.yaml`): bean soldiers; knight has a detachable helmet
  (hit zone head), sword and shield; walk, attack, hop.
- **Trebuchet** (`models/trebuchet.kiln.yaml`): rigid arm on an axle; fire.
- **Wall**, **tree**, **barrel**: set dressing.
