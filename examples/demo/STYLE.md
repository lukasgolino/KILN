# Style

## Look

- **Style file:** soft_toy (built-in preset)
- **Shapes:** big, simple, every edge rounded; body parts blended (0.03-0.06 m), details with crisp seams.
- **Proportions:** bean bodies ~1.1 m; stubby limbs; eyes 0.1 m white spheres with 0.04 m pupils.
- **Colours:** palette `toybox`; one strong colour per character, white/black/gold accents.
- **Materials:** plastic for toys, fabric for clothes, metal/gold accents, wood and stone for the siege set.
- **Lighting:** bright day, filmic, soft shadows, SSAO and a little glow (look kit `looks/soft_toy.tscn`).

## Rules

- Metres; models stand on y = 0 and face +Z. Colours by palette name.
